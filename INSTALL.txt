PCC Passport Autofill Helper

1. Download or clone this repository and extract it if needed.
2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions
3. Enable Developer mode.
4. Click Load unpacked.
5. Select the extracted project folder.
6. Open the relevant form and open the extension popup.
7. Select a text-based passport PDF and click Extract passport info.
8. Review all extracted values, then click Fill current form.
9. Review the form again; the extension will not submit it.

Passport files and extracted values are processed locally. Never upload passport files, passwords, cookies, or other personal information to GitHub.

Scanned PDFs and image OCR are not supported. Extraction and field matching may be incomplete; verify everything.
